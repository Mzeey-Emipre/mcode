Why Cyclomatic Complexity Matters for AI Coding

Calculated as the number of decision branches (if, else, switch, for, catch, &&, ||) in a function.

Complexity Score Risk Profile Recommended Action
1 – 10 Low risk (simple, readable) Ideal target for focused utility functions and pure logic.
11 – 20 Moderate risk Manageable with comprehensive unit test coverage.
21+ ("God function") High risk (difficult to test) Deconstruct into smaller subroutines and specialized helper functions.
